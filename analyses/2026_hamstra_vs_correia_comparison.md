## Findings

1. **Fertility capital sets the ceiling; salt decides how close a field gets to it.** Correia has far cleaner water chemistry (EC less than half of Hamstra's) and still trails by 6.5 T. Hamstra's decades of dairy loading — double the OM, season-long N mineralization, 2–4× the K, 3–10× the Zn — is the gap. Practices are held constant across ranches by the same operator; the soil bank is the variable.
2. **Salinity tolerance is conditional on buffer, not the EC number.** Hamstra 29 made 42.0 T (70% basis) at EC 2.88 on CEC 18 / OM 3.6 / free lime. Correia V7 made 30.8 at EC 1.55 on light, non-calcareous, low-OM ground. Same lab thresholds, opposite outcomes. Interpret every EC reading against CEC, OM, and carbonate buffer.
3. **V15 is the exception that proves the rule.** The only Correia field built like Hamstra ground (CEC 20.9) is the only one performing at Hamstra levels (2-yr avg 40.5 @70). OM/CEC is the ceiling variable on both ranches.
4. **Residual-N behavior explains why the N rules differ by ranch.** Hamstra fields finish with 200–400 lb still banked (manure/lagoon mineralization); Correia crops run the tank to empty. Sidedress floor waivers are safe on Hamstra 300+ lb fields and unsafe at Correia (V15's 2025-vs-2026 budget comparison, locked-decisions §3). Both rules are correct — they're soil-bank rules, not ranch preferences.
5. **The sulfur finding is Correia-specific and now soil-test-conditional on both ranches.** Sulfate LOW on 6/7 Correia samples vs adequate on 5/6 Hamstra samples. Sulfur at sidedress pays where SO4-S tests low; it would be wasted money at Hamstra. (Hamstra Fld 25, SO4-S 35 LOW, is the one Hamstra field where the Correia sulfur play applies — via its 1.5 T gypsum.)
6. **Leaching is proven on-farm and transfers.** Hamstra 9: Na 30.2 → 18.5 %SPe in 7 months after a January pre-irrigation + 9 water passes; made the ranch's first 40+ crop. This is the direct evidence base for the Correia winter-leach program (locked-decisions §1) and for the pre-plant leach on Hamstra 11's valve ends.
7. **Where the money goes, by ranch:** Hamstra → water/salt management (leach scheduling, gypsum-with-water, dry-down timing); fertility dollars are nearly all avoidable. Correia → building the bank (concentrated 20 T manure per priority queue, Zn on the ≤1.2 fields, K replacement, P on V14/V8E); salt dollars only on V7/A-block.
