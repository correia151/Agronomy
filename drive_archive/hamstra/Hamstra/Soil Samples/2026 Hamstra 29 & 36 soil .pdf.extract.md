OM from compost or manure and/or cover crops will supply organic nutrients, stimulate microbial activity & nutrient recycling, enhance soil structure and benefit moisture retention. 1 percent OM is equivalent to 40,000 lbs OM per acre-foot of soil. Compost/manure commonly have 50% OM. 40 tons of compost/ac will provide 20 tons of OM, increasing OM by 1.0%. Field 12-36 areas have acceptable Alkaline & Neutral pH values within ideal ranges and are non-calcareous, no free lime is present. Avoid further pH reduction. Field 03-29 has an Alkaline pH value and is strong calcareous, significant free lime is present. Acidic reactions will adjust pH and react with free lime. The soil pH and the presence of free lime may be greater in the sub-surface soil depths. Investigation of the sub-soil depths is advised to fully determine lime presence. Acidic reactions from Sulfur and/or acidic fertilizer materials will reduce pH and dissolve free lime which will liberate & sustain Ca solubility & also enhance nutrient availability. Common soil sulfur will react over an extended period of 2 to 5 years. Tiger 90 sulfur material will provide a more immediate reaction & benefits. Maintain soluble salts (EC) above 0.6 dS/m to sustain water penetration. Total salt concentrations above 4.0 dS/m are considered to be excessive. The presence of significant to high soluble Calcium and also Sulfate-S may reflect past acidification efforts and/or gypsum treatments. Maintain soluble Ca greater than TWICE soluble Mg & Na. Supply Ca from high grade sources as needed. Elevated Ca salts can be tolerated at higher salinity thresholds. Maintaining soluble Ca will benefit water penetration, drainage & also enhance nutrient interactions and nutrient availability. 100% Gypsum contains 24% Ca & 17% Sulfur as Sulfate, 1 ton will supply 480 lbs Ca & 340 lbs SO4-S. Gypsum has an alkaline pH and can contain trace amounts of Lime and Boron. The Sulfate-S form of Sulfur is not acidifying, Sulfate is an essential nutrient required for the crop to utilize Nitrogen in amino acids & proteins development. Zn is not considered an economically essential nutrient for Alfalfa. Incorporated pre-plant Zn rates are listed as lbs actual Zn/ac from ZnSO4. Chelated Zinc materials are also effective. Maintain Zn for row crop rotations. Boron soil treatments of 1 to 3 lbs actual Boron/acre may be considered. Solubor contains 18% Boron. Trace Boron may in irrigation water and in gypsum amendments.
