1\. Hamstra Fld 03-28 0 0 0 2 LOW 1.0 1.0 Alkaline. High free lime. Acidify to react with free lime. Moderate (EC). Supply soluble Ca to benefit infiltration.

Alfalfa performs better with neutral to alkaline pH conditions. Legumes have a symbiotic relationship with rhizobium bacteria to fix nitrogen. Rhizobium activity decreases in acidic soils. Acceptable Alkaline pH value within ideal ranges. Calcareous, significant free lime is present. The soil pH and the presence of free lime may be greater in the sub-surface soil depths. Investigation of the sub-soil depths is advised to fully determine lime presence. Elevated pH and calcareous soil conditions may restrict the availability of Phosphate & Zinc and may also inhibit Iron interactions, inducing Iron chlorosis symptoms. Acidic reactions from Sulfur and/or acidic fertilizer materials will reduce pH and dissolve free lime which will liberate & sustain Ca solubility & also enhance nutrient availability. Common soil sulfur will react over an extended period of 2 to 5 years. Tiger 90 sulfur material will provide a more immediate reaction & benefits. It requires 3 tons of Sulfur to neutralize 1% Calcium Carbonate (CaCO3 "Free Lime"). Three tons of Sulfuric Acid will provide the same reaction as 1 ton of Sulfur. Maintain soluble salts (EC) above 0.6 dS/m to sustain water penetration. Total salt concentrations above 4.0 dS/m are considered to be excessive. Moderate salt concentration (EC) is present. Maintain soluble Calcium (Ca) greater than TWICE soluble Magnesium (Mg) & soluble Sodium (Na). Acidic reaction from sulfur will supply soluble Ca. Supply soluble Ca from high grade sources as needed. Elevated soluble Ca salts can be tolerated at higher salinity thresholds. Gypsum will supply soluble Ca and Sulfate & provide some short-term pH buffering. Irrigation delivered high grade soluble Ca will benefit infiltration, apply as needed. 100% Gypsum contains 24% Ca & 17% Sulfur as Sulfate, 1 ton will supply 480 lbs Ca & 340 lbs SO4-S. Gypsum has an alkaline pH and can contain trace amounts of Lime and Boron. The Sulfate-S form is not acidifying, Sulfate is an essential nutrient required for the crop to utilize Nitrogen in amino acids & proteins development. ALFALFA: 10 T/ac annual yield removes 120 lbs P2O5 and 600 lbs K2O/ac. Incorporated pre-plant Zn rates are listed as lbs actual Zn/ac from ZnSO4. Chelated Zinc materials are also effective. Maintain Zn for row crop rotations. Boron soil treatments of 1 to 3 lbs actual Boron/acre may be considered. Solubor contains 18% Boron. Trace Boron may in irrigation water and in gypsum amendments. Multiply ppm NO3-N (Nitrate-Nitrogen) by 6 to convert to lbs N/ac. Estimated Nitrogen Release (ENR) calculates the potential N release from organic matter. Calculate ENR by multiplying TN x 0.08 or 0.16 = ENR lbs/ac-ft. Alternative calculation: %OM x 4,000,000 = lbs OM/ac-12 inch. Lbs OM/ac-12 in x 0.05 = Total N/ac-12 in. Expect 2 to 4% of the calculated Total N to become available during the season. Blended irrigation with 50% dairy lagoon water & 50% fresh water may deliver 50 lbs N, 50 lbs K2O & 25 lbs P2O5 per acre-inch (27,154 gallon).
