Maintain soluble Calcium (Ca) greater than TWICE soluble Magnesium (Mg) & soluble Sodium (Na). The presence of significant to high soluble Calcium and also Sulfate-S may reflect past acidification efforts and/or gypsum treatments. Supply soluble Ca from high grade sources as needed.
