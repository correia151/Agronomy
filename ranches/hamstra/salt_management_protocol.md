# Hamstra — Salt Management & Emergence Protocol (rev. 1, 08-11-26)

Purpose: fix hot-weather emergence failure and uneven stands on high-salt fields, working within the operating constraint that **manure water must go on with the pre-irrigation most years**. Companion to Hamstra_Soil_Intelligence_2026.md and the fields 11/14/17 paired sampling protocol.

## 1. The 2026 diagnosis (what the data showed)

**Symptom:** short, stressed, uneven corn concentrated where water stands; worst in June heat. 2026 bad fields: **14, 17, 11, 16**. Best early field: **C1**; also strong: 6, C2, 31.

| 16 | Bad | **~547 lb — highest on ranch** | 1359 | Got 75 units at sidedress despite VT zero |

1. **Seed-zone salt concentration at emergence.** Nitrate is a salt: 300–550 lb residual + sodium + June heat = concentrated soil solution wicked to the bed surface exactly where the seed sits. Best-looking field (C1) = cleanest profile; worst (16) = highest.
2. **Manure water timing.** Lagoon water at pre-irrigation/watering-up delivers N-K-Na salt to the seed zone with no leaching interval. Prior evidence: 2024 notes — "18 irrigated manure water only and got burnt coming up"; "preirrigated field w manure did bad."
3. **Surface sealing → standing water → scald.** Sodium-dispersed surface seals, water ponds at valve/tail ends, ponded water in heat scalds seedlings. This is the standing-water-where-corn-is-short signature.
4. **Variety stacking.** All great fields = **1759** (proven hot-dirt emerger). Bad fields = 1608 and 1359, with 1608 sitting on heavy-manure ground against Eric's own note. "Everything the same" wasn't — the weakest emergers were on the saltiest ground.

Field 17 caveat: low residual N — its stress runs through the sodium/infiltration channel (valve-end lagoon delivery), not nitrate. Confirms the 11/14/17 diagnostic focus.

## 2. The protocol (in application order)

**Operating constraint honored: manure water stays in the pre-irrigation program.** The fix is sequencing + water-run calcium + fall iron, not eliminating lagoon water.

### Fall (post-harvest, ground dry)
1. **Chisel/rip the ponding checks** — valve ends and tail ends where water stood, on flagged fields. Fall only; never spring-chisel ahead of corn (dries and roughens the seedbed). Note: field 11 has been on the no-chisel list since 2022 — take it off for the problem checks.
2. **Surface-apply gypsum on those same checks** after ripping, ahead of winter water. Purpose is infiltration (keeping the surface flocculated so leach water enters), NOT yield — the 2022–2025 analysis shows no corn-yield response to gypsum; this is its one real job here.
3. **Post-harvest soil sample = next spring's pre-plant map** (see §4).

### Winter / pre-plant
4. **Manure water EARLY in the pre-irrigation window; FRESH water as the LAST pass before planting.** The fresh flush pushes salts below the seed zone. Evidence both directions: field 18's 2024 manure-water-only watering-up burned; field 9's January 2026 fresh pre-irrigation cut Na 30.2→18.5 %SPe en route to the ranch's first 40+ T crop.
5. **February EC walk** with a handheld meter (Hanna GroLine class, ~$200–400): top 6" at valve end and tail end of every corn field, ~20 min/field. Flags the specific checks that get steps 6–7. No lab lag.

### Planting / first irrigations (flagged fields)
6. **Water-run calcium in the pre-irrigation and/or first irrigation:** solution gypsum (injection machine) or **calcium thiosulfate (CaTs)**. Dissolved Ca arrives at the surface with the water — counteracting the sodium the lagoon water delivers, in the same pass. **Do NOT use CAN-17 on these fields** (17% N on 300+ lb residual ground = adding salt to a salt problem).
7. **Variety placement:** 1759 (or the proven best hot-dirt emerger of the year) on flagged salt fields. **1608 permanently off heavy-manure ground** (Eric's note, now data-backed). 
8. **Honor Valley Tech zeros.** No sidedress on 300+ lb fields (16/11/14-class). Saves money AND removes salt. C1 proves N *after* establishment on clean ground is fine — the issue is salt at emergence, not fertilizer per se.

### Standing intelligence
9. **Lagoon water panel** (added to the 11/14/17 sampling set): quantifies N/K/Na per acre-inch so manure-water salt import is a known number, not a guess.
10. **Add field 16 to the paired sampling protocol** (good spot vs bad spot, 0–6" and 6–24", tissue with Na/Cl).

## 3. Why not other amendments (quick reference)
- **CAN-17:** soluble Ca but 17% N — contraindicated on high-residual fields (see §2.6). Fine agronomically on clean-profile ground needing N + Ca together.
- **Elemental sulfur / acids:** slow (2–5 yr) and aimed at free lime; only 29-class calcareous ground justifies it. Not an emergence fix.
- **More broadcast gypsum tonnage:** no yield response in 13-yr record; keep the 1 T every-other-year maintenance clock and put the targeted effort into water-run Ca + checks that pond.
- **Humic/"salt-buster" products:** no on-ranch evidence; if trialed, run as a half-field strip against the water-run gyp standard, never ranch-wide.
