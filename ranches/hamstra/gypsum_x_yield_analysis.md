## Interpretation (consistent with the rulebook)

1. **Confirms saline-not-sodic.** Na%CEC 2–4% ranch-wide means gypsum has no exchangeable sodium to displace; leaching is the reclamation lever (field 9: 30.2 → 18.5 %SPe via winter water, first 40+ T crop). Gypsum's role is infiltration and Ca:Mg:Na maintenance — salt-burdened fields yielding at parity with clean fields is that role working, but it does not appear as a next-crop yield bump.
2. **Validates the 1 T every-other-year baseline and argues against escalation.** Rate increases should be chemistry-driven (e.g., field 25's failed Ca 2× rule + low sulfate), never yield-chasing.
3. **Where yield actually moves:** planting date and field identity swing 5–15 T in the same dataset; gypsum swings ~0. Marginal dollars go to early ground prep, leach water, and the alfalfa program before extra gypsum tons.
4. **Caveats:** 2024 gyp group includes several bad-emergence fields; variety not controlled in the paired test; wheat-crop response (stand, tillering on crusting soils) not evaluated here — this analysis is corn-response only.

## Proposed experiment
Same design as the N-floor strips: on one mid-salt double-crop field in its "on" year, skip gypsum on half. One season, one scale ticket per half, settles the corn-response question with field/variety/season fully controlled.
