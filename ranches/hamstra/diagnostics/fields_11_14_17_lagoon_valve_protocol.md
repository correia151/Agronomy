## Why these three fields
The 2026 bad-emergence cluster. All three sit on the same signature:
**valve-end lagoon delivery → sodium loading → surface sealing → standing
water → scald in June heat**, stacked with high residual nitrate on 11 and 14.

## Diagnostic design (paired sampling)
For each field, sample a **bad spot (valve/tail end where water stood) vs an
adjacent good spot**, at **0–6" and 6–24"**, plus **tissue with Na/Cl**.
Field 16 is added to the set (good vs bad spot, same depths — its channel is
nitrate-salt, giving the contrast case).

Submit a **lagoon water panel with the same set** — quantifies N/K/Na per
acre-inch so manure-water salt import is a known number, not a guess.
